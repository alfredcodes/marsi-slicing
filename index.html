01 Our Story

A taste of the
Himalayas

Marsi Thakali brings the authentic flavors of Thakali cuisine to the heart of Lalitpur. Our restaurant is dedicated to preserving the rich culinary traditions of the Thakali people from the Mustang district.

Every dish tells a story—from our signature Dal Bhat sets to our flavorful curries and handmade momos. We invite you to experience the warmth of Nepali hospitality.
